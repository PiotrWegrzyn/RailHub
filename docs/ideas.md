# Ideas for later

Ideas the player raised that are not built or agreed yet.

## Land past the place's map

Buying lots next to the map is built (README, Maps), but only as far as the map of the game's
place reaches: a lot with no land on it is never offered. The player asked for no limit at all,
which needs lots past the place's map made from the world's map data, the way a random place's
map is (`railroads.mapgen.world_files`).

## A goods market

A market window listing the price of every good in the world and how it changes over time, as
the fuel market already does for coal, wood and diesel: prices pulled up by demand and down by
supply. Freight would pay a share of these market prices, and the player's own goods would sell
at them. Raised on 2026-11-18.

## A balanced test map

One map made for balancing, a cross-section of the world: four quarters split by rivers, each a
different land (green and temperate, desert, tundra, savanna), each with a fair share of streams,
rivers, hills, mountains, sea and lakes. Growth and the economy would be tuned on it once the
tuning on Poland is done. Raised on 2026-11-21.

## Spare supply draws its users

Where much of a good is made and nobody nearby uses it, the works that would use it come up more
likely: lots of coal and no steel mill makes a steel mill likelier, many cattle make a dairy
likelier. Raised on 2026-11-21.

## Fisheries merge into ports

Three fisheries in a neighbourhood merge into a port once the neighbourhood holds at least four
people per land hex, not counting mountains and water. Raised on 2026-11-21.
