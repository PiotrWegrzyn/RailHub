# Ideas for later

Ideas the player raised that are not built or agreed yet.

## Buying map fragments

Make the starting map a bit smaller and let the player buy more land next to it, without limit.
A bought fragment is grown on its own up to the year it is bought, and only then revealed, so it
arrives as developed as the rest of the map. Raised on 2026-11-18, together with the question of
how much a bigger map costs in speed.

Prices, as the player put them: each fragment costs F(N) × $1,000, the Fibonacci number F(N),
starting at N = 14 ($377,000) and going up by one with every purchase ($610,000, then $987,000,
and so on). The player can always buy in four directions.
