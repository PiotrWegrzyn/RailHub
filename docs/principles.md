# Guiding principles

The ideas every rule of the economy should follow.

1. **The land decides where producers come up.** Farms, orchards, fisheries, mines, quarries and
   mills appear more often where the land suits them, and stand for as long as the land is theirs.
2. **Producers decide where consumers can survive.** Homes, shops and works live where what they
   need is made within reach, and die out where it is not.
3. **The player earns by adding value to the economy, filling the gaps in its markets.** Trains
   pay for bringing goods to where they are missing, and what they bring makes the places they
   serve grow.

## The rules that follow from them

- Buildings sell what they make to the buildings around them on their own, up to ten cells away,
  each cell of the way losing a tenth of what was sent, the nearest first. Stations take and hand
  out first.
- Buildings come up at their base chance anywhere the land allows, and breed by how satisfied they
  are, the more the more of what draws them stands around.
- Producers of what the land gives never starve to death; fields go only when a town grows over
  them. Homes starving for ten years die out, everything else after thirty.
- A processor is satisfied only by what it makes and sells, never by a full stockpile.
- Value comes from scarcity, not distance.
